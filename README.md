# TaniJogo

Repo for AI for Business Hackathon.

## Hackathon Description

Forward, organised by DSCubed and RAID, is a 48-hour hackathon focused specifically on applying AI in a business setting. Participants will have the opportunity to meet other passionate students, form a team, receive mentoring from experienced mentors, and develop a project to tackle real-world challenges relating to business and AI.

Teams of 2–5 people will work through one of four challenge tracks throughout the hackathon and are given 48 hours to bring their projects to life. During this period, participants will have access to coworking spaces and experienced mentors for support.

Sponsored by ElevenLabs and Eleno, leading companies in developing AI solutions for businesses, the total prize pool is valued at $3000+, along with additional prizes including free ElevenLabs access. There are also potential internships and job opportunities available at Eleno.

Through this event, participants will be able to gain invaluable experience connecting and networking with like-minded people and industry professional judges, and have a finished product that they can be proud of.
